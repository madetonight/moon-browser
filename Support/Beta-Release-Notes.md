# Moon 0.4.8 beta

Meet Nebula. Your voice, in Moon.

- Dictate into text fields with on-device speech and review before inserting.
- Control tabs, navigation, searches and browser tools with spoken commands.
- Open Nebula from the sidebar or Edit menu. Hold Control–Shift–Space to speak; customize the shortcut in Settings.
- Language models are managed by macOS. Audio and transcripts stay out of browsing history.

Apple silicon, macOS 26 or later. One signed DMG serves installation and in-app updates. This Moon beta is Apple Development signed and is not Apple-notarized. The optional ActivityWatch companion is distributed separately by its authors.
