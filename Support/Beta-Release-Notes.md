# Moon 0.4.17 beta

Liquid Glass, edge to edge.

- Turn on Liquid Glass in Appearance → Window for a continuous window background. The glass takes on blurred colors from behind the window, with a graphite finish in dark mode and soft pearl in light mode.
- In Glass mode, Settings uses thin dividers and the browsing layout has no inset page frame or separate glass outlines around the tabs.
- Delete Space takes effect immediately. The default space stays protected.

Apple silicon, macOS 26 or later. One signed DMG serves installation and in-app updates. This Moon beta is Apple Development signed and is not Apple-notarized.
