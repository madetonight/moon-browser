# Moon 0.4.19 beta

- The close-tab confirmation follows Moon’s design. Close Tab comes first. Return closes the tab and Escape keeps it open.
- YouTube’s player can complete its startup requests while Shield is on.
- Moon’s floating video window follows the new video when a page replaces its player.

Apple silicon, macOS 26 or later. One signed DMG serves installation and in-app updates. This Moon beta is Apple Development signed and is not Apple-notarized.
