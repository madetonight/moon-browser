# Moon 0.4.16 beta

Smoother switching between Spaces.

- The sidebar follows your swipe and settles into place. Swipe left from the last space to make a new one, or swipe right to go back.
- Click a page dot or use a keyboard shortcut for the same slide. When multiple spaces are open, the sidebar names the current space above its tabs.
- Folder icons make collapsible Tab Groups easier to spot.

Apple silicon, macOS 26 or later. One signed DMG serves installation and in-app updates. This Moon beta is Apple Development signed and is not Apple-notarized.
