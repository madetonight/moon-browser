# Moon 0.4.7 beta

- Give selected sidebar tabs a solid, consistent highlight across sites and split tabs.
- Keep the highlight at the configured row height when switching or pressing tabs.
- Show optional reading progress as a small underline on the active sidebar tab.

Apple silicon, macOS 26 or later. One signed DMG serves installation and in-app updates. This Moon beta is Apple Development signed and is not Apple-notarized. The optional ActivityWatch companion is distributed separately by its authors.
