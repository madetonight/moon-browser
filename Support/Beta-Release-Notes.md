# Moon 0.4.21 beta

- Switch between **Search** and **Ask AI** on the Moon home page. Press Return in Ask AI to open your question in ChatGPT.
- Click **New tab** in the sidebar to open a fresh tab each time. **⌘T** keeps the floating address field when enabled.
- Extensions, Bookmarks, Downloads, Translate, and Orbit are back in the sidebar.

This Apple Development-signed beta supports Apple silicon Macs on macOS 26 or later. One DMG serves manual installation and in-app updates. The beta has no Apple notarization.
