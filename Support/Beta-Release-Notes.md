# Moon 0.4.10 beta

Nebula. Just say it.

- Hold Option–Space, speak, then release to control Moon. The shortcut is customizable in Settings.
- Say “close all my YouTube tabs”, “switch to Work”, “summarize this page” or “tidy my tabs”. Closing tabs asks for confirmation and preserves pinned tabs.
- A compact native voice indicator stays inside the Moon window, with live transcription, dictation mode and speech-language controls.
- Speech runs on this Mac. Page summaries use your configured AI provider; enable Ollama in Settings → AI.

Apple silicon, macOS 26 or later. One signed DMG serves installation and in-app updates. This Moon beta is Apple Development signed and is not Apple-notarized. The optional ActivityWatch companion is distributed separately by its authors.
